DEAD AIR

WKRT CHANNEL 9 · NOV 19 1994 · TEST BUILD

The station went off the air mid-broadcast. You and three crew mates have until 6:00 AM to get it back on. One of them is not who they look like. Only the camcorder shows its real face.

QUEST
Left stick: move
Right stick: snap turn
Right trigger: use what you point at
Right grip: flashlight on/off
Left trigger: record (camcorder)
X or A: call a crew mate to you
Walkie screen is on your left wrist
DESKTOP
WASD: move, mouse: look
Click or E: use
Hold right mouse: raise camcorder
Hold R: record
F: flashlight, Q: call a crew mate
Esc: free the mouse
Win: do the 3 jobs and reach the van, or record the Host's real face for 3 seconds and burn the tape in the furnace. Headphones recommended.
